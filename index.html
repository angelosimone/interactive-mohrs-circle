Instructions

  • Press r or the Reset button to start.
  • Drag the red or green marker to rotate the stress state.
  • The physical rotation θ shows the minimum rotation from the reference configuration.
  • Sign convention used in the Mohr plane (σn-τn plane): positive normal stress corresponds to tensile normal stress; positive shear stress corresponds to clockwise rotation of the material element.
  • Press r or the Reset button to return to the reference (unrotated) configuration.
